**How the pilots shaped the formal design** (listed as they are):
- Sizes chosen from pilot 2: WHERE uses depth 8 × 12 rows, depth 32 × 6 rows and depth 64 × 6 rows; depth 16 produced almost no errors and was dropped. CHECK uses depth 32.
- Effort: medium only. In the pilot the effect appeared at both low and medium.
- Query writing (task B) was at ceiling in the pilots. It is stated here in advance that it is tested as a "writing is not reading" contrast (H5); items are not changed to chase errors.
- All formal items are newly generated and do not overlap the pilot pools: task A uses seed 20261120 (0 of 680 items overlap the pilot pools); task B uses new parameters and instances (seeds 20261121/20261122) with the same item types as v2/v3.

## 3. Measures
**Row categories** (ids-type WHERE and CHECK items; "a row is in the answer" means the query returns its id).
- **Classical row:** all five semantics (sql, wk, nf, nt, eq) agree on the row.
- **Absorbing row:** sql and wk disagree, i.e. SQL absorbs the NULL and "NULL poisons everything" flips the row.
- **Unknown row:** sql and wk agree but some other collapse rule does not, i.e. the row's condition is UNKNOWN in SQL.

**Row error:** the model's judgment of a row (in the answer or not) differs from the gold (sql semantics).

**Missing answers:** truncated, refused, API-error and unparsable answers contribute no rows and are counted per model × arm × cell. A cell with more than 20% missing is declared not testable, left out of every test that uses it, and reported.

**Inference:** item-cluster bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261117, one-sided. p = (1 + #resampled differences ≤ 0) / (valid resamples + 1). Holm correction over the three main models, α = 0.05.

## 4. Hypotheses (main models Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, each decided separately)
- **H1 (main, WHERE).** The error rate on absorbing rows of S2 items exceeds the error rate on unknown rows of S1 items. Data: arms A and A64, three cells pooled. The two groups of items are resampled independently.
- **H2 (within items).** In S2 WHERE items, the error rate on absorbing rows exceeds the error rate on classical rows of the same items. Items resampled once (paired). Rules out "S2 items are merely harder to compute".
- **H3 (CHECK).** The H1 contrast on arm C. CHECK rejects only FALSE, so the direction is opposite to WHERE.
- **H4 (rules stated).** On the same S2 items, the absorbing-row error rate is lower in arm R (rules) than in arm A (plain). Only the A items that also appear in R; paired by item.
- **H5 (writing ≠ reading).** In task B, the share of trap items whose query is correct on every NULL instance is at least 0.90; plain and bare pooled.

**Decision.** H1–H4 hold if the Holm-adjusted p < 0.05. H5 holds if the point estimate ≥ 0.90; the Wilson 95% lower bound is reported. Hypotheses that fail are reported as they are in the results.

## 5. Secondary analyses (no multiplicity correction; labelled secondary in the paper)
